About Ceylon Escapes

Discover Sri Lanka with context, care, and curiosity.

Ceylon Escapes is a tourism website created to present Sri Lanka’s destinations, culture, wildlife, food, stays, and travel experiences in one welcoming guide.

Our project

This website demonstrates destination marketing, tourism product development, sustainable travel communication, and digital visitor information design in the context of Sri Lanka.

Use the destination directory, travel guide, and experience pages to plan an informed journey while respecting local communities, cultural heritage, and natural environments.
