Travel with care

Responsible Tourism in Sri Lanka

Thoughtful travel protects the places and communities that make the island special.

Simple principles

Choose locally owned stays and guides, respect sacred sites and dress requirements, reduce single-use plastics, keep a respectful distance from wildlife, and leave natural and cultural places exactly as you found them.

Support conservation fees, follow park instructions, ask before photographing people, and learn a few words of Sinhala or Tamil. Small decisions help tourism create lasting local value.
